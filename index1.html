Our Story

A Family Legacy
of Excellence

AlSharif Marketing Association is a fully Egyptian, family-owned company that has been operating at the highest level of the luxury beauty industry since its formal establishment in 1998 — built upon a foundation of over 35 years of collective expertise in perfumes and cosmetics.

From a vision of bringing the world's most coveted fragrance and beauty brands to the Egyptian market, AlSharif has grown into the country's foremost distributor, operating a multi-channel business spanning retail, wholesale distribution, duty-free, and e-commerce.

With a team of 300+ dedicated professionals and more than 31 retail locations, we continue to connect Egyptians with the world's finest luxury experiences.

Our Journey

A History of
Ambition

1988
The Foundation

The seeds of AlSharif are planted — deep passion and professional expertise in fragrances and cosmetics begins shaping what will become Egypt's finest luxury distributor.

1998
AlSharif M.A. Established

Al-Sharif Marketing Association is officially incorporated as a family business in Egypt, beginning with exclusive distribution agreements for premium international brands.

2009
AlSharif Beauté Launched

The proprietary retail chain AlSharif Beauté is born — a specialized store concept dedicated to international brands of fragrances, cosmetics, watches, and leather goods.

2015
Duty-Free Expansion

AlSharif enters duty-free retail, establishing a presence in EgyptAir shops, EgyptAir in-flight, and EFSCO — reaching international travelers at Egyptian airports.

2019
E-Commerce Launch

alsharifbeaute.com launches — an ambitious digital strategy that rapidly scales to 7-figure revenues, making luxury accessible across every corner of Egypt.

2020+
31 Branches & Beyond

With 31+ retail stores, 300+ employees, and a rapidly growing online presence, AlSharif continues its mission: bringing the world's finest to Egypt.
